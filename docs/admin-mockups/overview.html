Why these five and not more

Each one compares a derived store against the thing it is derived from, and each has a repair. Nothing is listed here that no instrument in this repo measures: a card reading "unknown" is a roadmap wearing the costume of an instrument, and a reader cannot tell one from a measured card at a glance.

What a disagreement between the first two figures means

D1 is derived from the repository, so those two numbers are supposed to be equal. When they are not, the repository is right and D1 is behind. The sync at the top of this page is the repair, and it runs at every ship as well, so a gap is usually a window rather than a fault.
