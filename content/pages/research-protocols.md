These are the laboratory protocols used in Dustin Edwards's lab at Tarleton State University: the phage methods taught in the SEA-PHAGES course, and PCR primers with their reaction and cycling conditions. Each protocol has its own page with the full method, a printable bench sheet and a markdown copy. They are written for students in the lab and for anyone running the same methods elsewhere.

Search the library below, or narrow it by method, organism, target or course. Every filtered view has its own address, so a link to it shows the same list. The same rows are available as [CSV](/research/protocols.csv) and [JSON](/research/protocols.json), and the whole library as [markdown](/research/protocols.md).

## How to cite a protocol

Cite the protocol's own page: the title, "Dustin Edwards Lab, Tarleton State University", the address and the date you accessed it. A protocol's page names the papers it follows, so cite those for the method itself.
