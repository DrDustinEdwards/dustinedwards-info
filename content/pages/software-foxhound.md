## Merchants with failed subscription payments

Foxhound is for merchants whose Stripe subscription payments fail. It retries the failed recurring charge and, when a retry will not fix the decline, escalates to drafted customer sequences. It takes a share of what it recovers and nothing otherwise, replaces Recova, and is live at [foxhoundapp.com](https://foxhoundapp.com).
