This is the list of bacteriophages isolated from soil and water by undergraduates in the Tarleton State University SEA-PHAGES course in Stephenville, Texas. It has {{phages.count}} phages found from {{phages.firstYear}} to {{phages.lastYear}}. The {{phages.host.smegmatis.count}} phages from {{phages.host.smegmatis.years}} were isolated on [*Mycobacterium smegmatis*](/research/lab/strains/smegmatis); from {{phages.host.foliorum.firstYear}} on, the lab has used [*Microbacterium foliorum*](/research/lab/strains/foliorum), the host for {{phages.host.foliorum.count}} of them. Each phage has its own entry below with its host, the year it was found, the Texas county it came from, its PhagesDB record, and its genome announcement where one has been published. For how these phages were found, purified and prepared for sequencing, see [phage discovery](/teaching/phage-discovery). The genome papers are also listed under [publications](/research/publications).
