Dustin Edwards builds software as well as doing the virology. This page is the index: three groups, and each product has a short page that links out rather than replacing the product's own site.

## Products

- [Foxhound](/software/foxhound) recovers failed Stripe subscription payments. It replaces Recova.
- [Foxing](/software/foxing) is a public-domain reading application.
- [Foxing Edu](/software/foxing-edu) is a separate product built on Foxing and sold to schools.

## Sites

- [Germomics](/software/germomics) is his narrative podcast on microbiology and history.
- TXASM is the Texas Branch ASM. Dustin Edwards is the branch archivist. [txasm.org](https://txasm.org)

## Infrastructure

- [Capsid](/software/capsid) is the namespaced document store this site's rulings live in.
- [Enarratio](/software/enarratio) is an open-source library for accessible, server-rendered charts. It is in early alpha.
- [Carrel](/software/carrel) is the private writing hub where this site's articles are written.
