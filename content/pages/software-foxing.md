## Readers of public-domain books

Foxing is a reading application for anyone reading public-domain books. It carries a curated library from Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg, with progress tracking and social features, because anyone can have the same books and the work is in the curation, the typography, and the reading state. It is live at [foxing.app](https://foxing.app), and [Foxing Edu](/software/foxing-edu) is a separate product built on Foxing and sold to schools.
