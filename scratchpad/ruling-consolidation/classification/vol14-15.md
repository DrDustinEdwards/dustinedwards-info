## Contradictions

1. **R12 against a gated sentence in the code.** R12 (and the vol 14 cache-arc ship entry) says recordTraffic in the gateway sees every page view, cached or not, labelled "requests, includes crawlers". `CACHE_SENTENCE` in `app/lib/admin/origin-requests.mjs` still says "Cached responses never reach the Worker, so they are not counted here", and `check:admin-ui` (line ~3548) asserts that sentence verbatim. The page and its gate describe the pre-cache-arc world. One of the two is wrong. Nobody has re-measured whether a Workers Cache HIT still invokes the gateway.
2. **R9 against advertise.ts.** R9 says the endpoint is "on the request's origin, derived, not hardcoded". `app/lib/webmention/advertise.ts` uses the `SITE_ORIGIN` constant on purpose and explains why. The code's reasoning is the later one, but no ruling amends R9.
3. **R6 merged into HR9, but HR9 exempts the admin plane.** R6 required the admin mention actions to work without script. Since R116 merged it into a law that exempts admin, the scriptless requirement no longer binds, and admin.mentions.tsx now depends on ConfirmDialog/showModal for delete (R54). The merge dropped content quietly. Not a live conflict between two STANDING rules, but the merge lost information.
4. There is mild tension between R29 ("anything that ends in Dustin's hands and is not a decision is a defect") and R131 ("Ship stays Dustin's"). They are compatible if ship counts as a decision. No other conflicts found among the STANDING rows (2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 19, 20, 23, 27, 29) or against HR8, HR16 and R131. The hook's d1 arm was updated for R131 at 62845cf.
