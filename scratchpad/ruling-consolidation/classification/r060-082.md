## Contradictions

1. **The redesign brief contradicts itself about R68, R73 and R82.** Its section "The five rulings moved here by ruling 116" says R67, R68, R72, R73, R82 "are law, not history" and "where the two differ, the ruling above is the one that was ratified". The same brief says ruling 117 "supersedes this brief wherever the two disagree" and withdraws bento. R68 (bento on /projects and /colophon), R73 (home Ask demonstration, no status numerals on /) and R82 (one filled primary per page) all conflict with 117 and 118. A cold reader of the brief gets two opposite answers. The brief's HARD CONSTRAINTS ("Bento grids on /projects and /colophon only", "the home page carries one live demonstration, the Ask form"), its LAYOUT ("/projects and /colophon may break to a grid"), its TWO KITS ("filled purple only on the one primary action per page") and its color table (brand for "the one primary button per page") repeat the superseded text.
2. **The brief's HEADER section still describes the purple bar** (`--surface-chrome`, `--on-chrome`, "Controls in the bar"), against 117 and the shipped paper header (1f06b03). Its last paragraph also still says "77 and 100 are SUSPENDED for the header and are not law while the suspension stands", while its 117 paragraph says 117 lifted that suspension. `app/app.css` still declares `--surface-chrome: #4f2d7f`, which 122 ordered deleted (outside this slice).
3. **The brief's TYPE section** (serif for "public headings 32px and up", "never on the logo") contradicts 117 and 123 (serif for titles and prose at every width). Superseded R80 is still printed as current there.
4. **Vol 19's "Design law a session needs cold" block** still lists 66, 70 amended by 74, and 71 (glass on theme toggle and search trigger) as standing. The same volume's later rulings 117, 123 and 124 overturn 70, 71 and 74. The brief also still says "Rulings 69 and 74 merged into 71 and 70 and stay in decisions-vol-19.md", as if they were live.
5. **Outside the slice, noticed:** ruling 118 item 5 ("build ONE static haze on a post figure", carried in the brief's Light section) contradicts ruling 124 ("figures are never lit"; lamp tokens "NEVER paint on a figure"). 124 is later and should win, but the brief states both. decisions-2026-09-13-session.md also still says "Glass is the field only" for Ask and that the lamp "ships on the /search field", both contrary to 117 and 124.
