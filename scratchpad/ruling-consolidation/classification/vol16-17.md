The code treats R58 as never having been ruled. One of the two needs correcting: either implement R58 or record that R58 was declined.

4. **R46 against the corpus, not another ruling.** R46 bans the "This article describes how to" opening; four published posts still open with it. The rule stands and is unapplied there.
5. **Vol 16's unnumbered view-transitions closure against R67/R125.** Vol 16 closed view transitions as refuted (1686751; `navigation: none` still at app/styles/motion-print.css:93, check:browser asserts no transition). R67 (redesign brief) and R125 list view transitions as allowed motion.
