## Contradictions

1. **103/105 against the live carried map.** 103 says "A row whose owner does not exist and is not scheduled is not carried, it is DEAD, and it is deleted", and 105 says a declined owner is closed evidence. The map in `check-invariants.mjs` still carries `--glass-fill-paper` for "the /search overlay glass, ruling 71" and four lamp tokens for "the lamp on the glass controls, ruling 74". Ruling 117 retired glass as a material for controls and 123 retired Paper, Glass, Light; R74 was merged into R70. By 103's own test those rows name owners that no longer exist.
2. **122 against 124 (outside my slice, touches 103).** 122 (vol 19): "The retired tokens are deleted NOW, not at the 2026-11-30 expiry... The lamp set, `--glass-fill-paper` and the chrome role tokens go... The carried map is emptied in the same change." 124, later in the same volume: glass is the pane over dialogs, image viewer and figure readouts, and "This gives the lamp set its first real consumer, so it is no longer a candidate for deletion." The later one wins, but 122's "emptied now" is also unapplied: the map still holds 44 rows. Whoever owns 122 and 124 should mark 122's deletion clause as superseded for the lamp set and `--glass-fill-paper`.
3. **101's literal wording against check:logo as shipped.** Vol 19 says "`check:logo`'s second fill binding stays RESTORED". c015986 (ruling 118.2) removed it and moved its guard to a component read. The intent holds; the wording in vol 19 is now false. I classed 101 as STANDING on its principle.
4. **93 listed standing in vol 19, retired by 116 in the same volume.** The later entry (116, plus retired-2026-09.md) wins, so 93 is STALE. Vol 19's Process list still shows it.
5. **84 "same strategy as Inter" against app.css.** Inter's normal face is `font-display: optional`; the serif is `swap` behind a size-adjusted fallback. Not a conflict between two rulings, but 84's wording no longer describes the file.
