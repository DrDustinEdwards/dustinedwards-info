The Inter .woff2 files this notice covers are the LATIN SUBSETS of the Inter
variable font, weight axis 100 to 900, upright and italic. They were taken from
the files fonts.gstatic.com was already serving to every visitor of this site,
so self-hosting changed who serves them and not what is served.

WHERE THEY LIVE, since 2026-08-21: app/fonts/ in the repository, emitted by the
build to /assets/ with a content hash in the filename. The hash is what lets
them be cached immutably for a year, because a URL whose contents cannot change
is the only kind that is safe to cache that way.

THIS NOTICE STAYS AT A STABLE URL on purpose. A hashed filename is not something
anyone can type or link to, and the licence has to be reachable by whoever
receives the fonts.
